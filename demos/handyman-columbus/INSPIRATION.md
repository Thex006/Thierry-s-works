# Inspiration: Columbus handyman demo (2D)

Rebuilt from scratch on 2026-11-22 (new deal, clean copy of the design kit, new page).

## Sources and what I took
- **ServiceTitan, "9 best handyman websites"** and **Webcitz, "best handyman website designs of 2026"**
  (found via web search): the best trade sites keep contact and the main action visible, use real job
  photos, and show a simple process. Taken: the quote button in the bar and the hero, a photo beside every
  section, prices and hours in plain rows, and a short "how we work" statement instead of a long process.
  Not taken: review carousels (we have no real reviews to show) and stock "smiling worker" heroes.
- **motionsites.ai**: its prompt library is mostly SaaS, AI and portfolio heroes. Nothing there fits a
  2D trade page; it goes to the 3D version.

## Why this look
Warm stone ground with a pine green accent (palette clay-pine, dealt for workshop trades): reads like plaster
and timber, plain and trustworthy for a local repair crew. Red Hat type: steady and friendly. Loads fast:
photos only, no WebGL, one scroll moment (the statement turns to ink word by word).
