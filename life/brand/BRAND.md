# Kitebright Studio brand

## Fonts
- **Emails:** Gmail default (Sans Serif / Arial), plain text, no font changes. Custom fonts don't
  survive other email apps and extra formatting looks like marketing to spam filters.
- **Our own materials** (invoices, PDFs, proposals, the studio site, templates we sell): **Inter**
  (Bold for headings, Regular for body). Same font as the logo.
- **Client demo sites** pick their own fonts via the taste skill; they're the client's brand, not ours.

## Colours
- Orange `#D66948` (wordmark text) · Kite gold `#F4B134` · Kite coral `#E07A55`
- Navy `#1B1F3B` · Muted blue-grey `#8088AA` (STUDIO)
