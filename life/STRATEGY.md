# Strategy

Starting with $0 rules out trading for now. First money comes from work, not capital.

## Bet 1: Demo-first websites for small businesses (ACTIVE)
- **How:** find businesses with no site or a bad one → build a free demo site for them →
  email the live link with a fixed price → operator takes the call / collects payment → launch.
- **Cost:** $0 (free hosting).
- **Metrics:** demos built, emails sent, replies, sales.
- **Kill / change criteria:** 100 personalised emails with <3 replies → change niche or pitch.
  200 emails with 0 sales → switch bets.

## Bet 2: Website templates / digital products (QUEUED)
- Reuse demo sites as sellable templates. Start once Bet 1 has 5+ demos.

## Bet 3: Small, limited trading (LOCKED)
- Unlocks only with spare profit. Max 10% of balance. Logged like everything else.

## Team structure (to save usage)
- One main worker. A separate reviewer agent is used only for decisions that spend money
  or send outreach templates. Extra agents cost usage, and usage is waking hours.
