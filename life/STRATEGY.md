# Strategy

Start: $0. So every bet must cost $0 to start and earn from skill and time, not capital.
Run up to 3 bets at once: outreach bets have waiting time (replies), so fill it with bets that don't.

## How bets are scored
Time to first $ · fits my strengths (code, writing, research, 50+ languages) · $0 to start · low risk.
Review every bet each session against its kill rule. Kill fast, double down on what pays.

## ACTIVE

### 1. Demo-first websites for small businesses
- Find businesses with no site or a bad one → build a personalised demo from a niche template →
  operator approves → email the live link with a fixed price (+ optional monthly care plan).
- Includes "redesign" pitches for businesses whose site is outdated or broken on phones.
- Skills: design-taste-frontend, redesign-existing-projects, imagegen-frontend-web.
- Kill rule: 100 emails with <3 replies → change niche/pitch. 200 emails with 0 sales → pause.

### 2. Open-source bounties
- Paid GitHub issues (Algora, IssueHunt, project-posted bounties). Pays for merged fixes. No outreach,
  no waiting on strangers to reply, fast feedback. Fills the gaps while bet 1 waits for replies.
- Only projects that allow AI-assisted contributions; disclose it. Payout account is the operator's.
- Kill rule: 10 submitted PRs with 0 merged → stop.

### 3. Digital templates (passive)
- Turn every niche demo into a sellable website template, plus Notion/spreadsheet templates.
  Sell on Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy ($0 to list). Each demo built for bet 1 becomes stock for bet 3.
- Kill rule: 10 listings with 0 sales after 30 days → change product type.

### Market & pricing (bet 1)
- **Start:** US home-service businesses (roofers, plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, landscapers) in mid-size
  cities (Tampa, Charlotte, Columbus…). One new customer is worth $500–$5,000 to them, so a site pays
  for itself fast, and they get less agency spam than NYC/LA.
- **Next (after 100 emails):** UK and Canada, then Germany/Netherlands written in their language.
- **Prices: research-based, not one-size.** Not everyone can pay $750. Before quoting, Scout and Ledger
  check the client's size (team, reviews, ticket size), their market's going rates, and competitors.
  Then Closer picks a tier:
  | Tier | What | Typical US price |
  |---|---|---|
  | Starter | 1-page site, mobile, contact form, their domain | $250–400 |
  | Standard | Up to 5 pages, booking/contact, Google listing linked | $600–900 |
  | Growth | Standard + online booking, SEO basics, extra language | $1,200–2,500 |
  | Care plan | Hosting, edits, monthly check | $29–79/month |
  - Offer a **payment plan** (2–3 parts) when price is the blocker. A yes at $350 beats a no at $750.
  - Floor: never below $200 for a site; Ledger must confirm the time spent is worth it.
  - Adjust for other markets by their local rates (lower-income markets get lower prices, not worse work).
  - Every price change is a team decision (see TEAM.md) and logged in LESSONS.md with the result.
- Payment: always via agreement + Stripe invoice. See PAYMENTS.md.
- Templates: $29–49 each. Bounties: whatever the issue pays ($50–$500+).

## QUEUED (start when a slot frees up)
- **Translation & localisation**: translate websites/apps/docs for small businesses. Huge strength.
- **Micro-tools**: tiny paid tools or browser extensions solving one problem, free hosting.
- **Bug bounties**: authorised programmes only (HackerOne, Bugcrowd), in scope, by the rules.
- **Freelance gigs via operator's profile**: writing, coding, translation on Contra/Upwork, AI use disclosed.

## LOCKED
- **Small trading**: only with spare profit, max 10% of balance.

## Skipped on purpose
- Paid surveys / click tasks (need a human), scraped lead lists (privacy), anything that needs fake reviews,
  fake personas or bulk spam.

## Team structure (to save usage)
- One main worker. A reviewer subagent only before spending money or sending a new pitch template.
